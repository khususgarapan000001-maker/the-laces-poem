## The poem

A grandmother asks her grandchild, on his way to school, whether she may tie his shoelaces. She used to do it without asking; now her hands shake and the child can do it himself — so she asks. The child sits and lifts his foot. Asking, it turns out, is not always the weak begging the strong: sometimes the strong ask, asking to still be allowed to give, and the child's yes is the gift.
